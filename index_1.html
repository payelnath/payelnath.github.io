Background

From circuits to supply chains
to the APIs freight moves on

I started with a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering — systems thinking baked in from first principles. Two years at Tata Consultancy Services running Commvault infrastructure for 2,000+ enterprise clients gave me production accountability from day one. An MBA at IBS Hyderabad (Marketing + Operations Management) redirected toward product and commercial roles.

Three years at Micron Technology embedded in global supply chain information systems — leading the enterprise migration from SAP APO to Blue Yonder Order Promising, designing supply planning algorithms, managing delivery across the US, Taiwan, and Singapore. Then E2Open as PO, seeing the same domain from the vendor side. Now at FedEx GCC as Senior PO on the Customer Experience Platform — sole product owner for eight enterprise APIs covering tracking, rating, pickup, document management, and commitment across EMEA and APAC.

What "inside the engine room" means
I don't own the product surface customers see. I own the API contracts that make the product surface possible — the Tracking API a logistics AI model calls, the Rating API a shipper uses to price a lane, the Commitment API a carrier relies on for SLA calculation. This is the data layer. Most PM candidates talk about it. I've owned its reliability, versioning, and governance for two years at Fortune 50 scale.
The non-traditional case — made plainly
I came from delivery, not product school. The gaps are real: no consumer-facing GTM, no formal metrics ownership, no AI product track record. This portfolio is the direct answer — not retrospective case studies, but working tools, real user research, deployed prototypes, and a PM build journal documenting every decision from discovery to iteration.
