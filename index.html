Background

I started in logistics technology and moved through supply chain planning systems into API platform ownership at FedEx GCC — a path that gave me domain depth most tech PMs never encounter: how global shipment networks actually function, why API governance matters at enterprise scale, and what it takes to ship product inside a complex, multi-stakeholder organisation.

My MBA from IBS Hyderabad layered in the business vocabulary — P&L framing, competitive strategy, stakeholder economics. My agile certifications (CSM, CSPO, A-CSPO, SAFe POPM) gave me the methodological rigour that scaled organisations run on. The raw material is there.

The Pivot

The line between Senior PO and Senior PM is real, but narrower than job descriptions suggest. PO work in scaled agile environments skews execution-heavy — sprint planning, backlog grooming, delivery accountability. PM work lives upstream: discovery, market framing, business case ownership, and strategic roadmap leadership.

I'm already operating upstream. I run discovery, shape capability roadmaps, and own strategic narratives with senior business stakeholders. What this portfolio is building is the public evidence of that — artifacts that show the thinking, not just the delivery record.

Target Senior PM roles at tech-first companies where supply chain, logistics, or API platforms are a core strategic surface — not an afterthought.

Building in Public

Most career pivots are announced after the fact. I'm doing the opposite — publishing real PM-level artifacts in the open across five strategic domains over 50 weeks. Not retrospective case studies. Not opinion pieces. Actual frameworks, analyses, and interactive tools — each one scoped and shipped like a product. The goal is dual: to demonstrate that my thinking already operates at PM level, and to deliberately learn the practices, tooling, and community norms of the tech PM world I'm moving into.

Demonstrating

  • Strategic product thinking that operates above sprint execution and backlog mechanics
  • Systems reasoning across the supply chain × API × platform intersection
  • Data-informed product decisions using Python, SQL, and product analytics frameworks
  • Independent end-to-end shipping: idea → information architecture → live artifact

Learning

  • Tech PM practices at product-led and growth-stage companies, outside enterprise SAFe
  • Modern AI tooling at the product operations and discovery layer
  • Consumer and SMB discovery methods versus enterprise stakeholder management
  • Building audience, credibility, and feedback loops through sustained public work
